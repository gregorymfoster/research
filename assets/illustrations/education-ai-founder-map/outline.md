# Illustration outline: The company inside an AI tutor

## Visual system

- Four editorial diagrams, each 3:2 landscape.
- Warm off-white paper, fine charcoal linework with slight hand variation, and one muted ochre accent.
- Sparse, purposeful labels in small clean hand lettering.
- Flat geometry and generous whitespace; no cards, badges, gradients, shadows, UI chrome, or stock-icon treatment.
- Figures and objects should feel like editorial marginalia rather than presentation clip art.

## Sequence

1. **Value ladder** — Show why generic study help has low pricing power and demonstrated competence has higher economic value.
2. **Founder opportunity map** — Plot eight company shapes by willingness to pay and retention/moat, preserving the article's judgment scores.
3. **Nursing competence journey** — Follow one learner from coursework through NCLEX, clinical onboarding, and continuing competence while an evidence thread persists.
4. **Competence flywheel** — Show the seven-step loop from paid practice to employer evidence and better future cases, with observed outcomes as the calibration hinge.
