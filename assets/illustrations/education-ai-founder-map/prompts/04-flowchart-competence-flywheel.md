Create a restrained editorial flywheel diagram for a serious research essay. Use a warm off-white paper background, fine charcoal-black linework with slight human irregularity, sparse small hand lettering, flat geometry, and one muted ochre accent. Match the supplied canonical reference images directly. It must feel drawn for an essay, not assembled from slide templates.

COMPOSITION
- Arrange seven small open nodes around an irregular but orderly clockwise loop.
- Connect them with one continuous thin charcoal line and understated arrowheads.
- Place a small pair of calibration scales at the center with the label BETTER DECISIONS.
- Use muted ochre only on OBSERVED OUTCOMES and the two connecting segments that enter and leave it; this is the hinge that turns activity into evidence.
- Each node can have one tiny line-drawn object that reinforces its meaning, but text remains primary.

NODE LABELS — clockwise, render exactly
- PAID PRACTICE
- UNAIDED ATTEMPTS
- OBSERVED OUTCOMES
- READINESS CALIBRATION
- PROGRAM ADOPTION
- EMPLOYER PATHWAYS
- BETTER FUTURE CASES

CENTER LABEL — render exactly
- BETTER DECISIONS

STYLE CONSTRAINTS
- No title, cards, badges, decorative banner, border, drop shadow, gradient, glow, 3D effect, photorealism, branded logos, speech bubbles, or UI elements.
- Do not add explanations, percentages, or extra labels.
- Keep all labels horizontal, distinct, and fully legible.
