Create a restrained editorial infographic for a serious research essay. Use a warm off-white paper background, fine charcoal-black ink lines with slight human irregularity, sparse small hand lettering, flat geometry, and one muted ochre accent. Match the supplied canonical reference images directly. This must look like an editorial illustration, not a slide, dashboard, or SaaS graphic.

COMPOSITION
- Draw a single ascending five-rung ladder or staircase moving from lower left to upper right.
- Each rung is a thin open line with ample whitespace, not a filled card.
- Let the bottom two rungs feel crowded and interchangeable; let the upper two feel more distinct and consequential.
- Use muted ochre only for the top rung and a thin upward thread connecting the rungs.
- Include a tiny notebook glyph near the bottom and a small verified-work sample or seal-like line drawing at the top, but do not use a badge shape.

LABELS — render exactly, with no other words
- GENERAL STUDY
- DISTRICT AI
- EXAM READINESS
- ROLE MASTERY
- DEMONSTRATED COMPETENCE

NUMBERS — render exactly beside the first three relevant rungs
- $36–$45 / YEAR
- $10 / STUDENT / YEAR
- $139–$389

STYLE CONSTRAINTS
- No title, legend, decorative banner, border, drop shadow, gradient, glow, 3D effect, photorealism, branded logos, speech bubbles, or UI elements.
- Do not add extra numbers or explanatory copy.
- Keep every label horizontal and fully legible.
