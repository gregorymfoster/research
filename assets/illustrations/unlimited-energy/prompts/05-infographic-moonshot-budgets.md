Minimal editorial bar chart, thin hand-drawn ink on warm off-white paper, precise like a chart in a serious essay. Match the visual language of the style reference. Compare five annual electricity quantities on one TWh-per-year scale, then show orbital kinetic energy separately. Do not add a title. Render every label and number below verbatim.

COMPOSITION: Five thin horizontal bars in descending order from one vertical baseline. Use outline bars with sparse diagonal hatching; fill only the data-center bar in muted ochre. Put the status word above the left end of each bar, the project label below it, and the value aligned at the right end. At lower right, separated by generous whitespace and one short vertical ink rule, place the launch note. Do not draw a box around it.
