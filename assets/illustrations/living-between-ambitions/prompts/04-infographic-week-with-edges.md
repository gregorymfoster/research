Primary request: Show a lightly structured week that contains stable anchors and genuine open space without becoming a crowded productivity calendar.

LAYOUT: One thin charcoal path crosses the wide 16:9 page from left to right in seven gentle segments, suggesting a week without drawing a calendar grid. Small tick marks indicate days but do not label Monday through Sunday. Most of the background remains empty.

ZONES:

- Place a cluster of two full circles plus one half circle beside 2 TO 3 EMBODIED MASTERY.
- Place two paired-circle marks beside 2 RECURRING SOCIAL.
- Place one ochre dot beside 1 CONTRIBUTION.
